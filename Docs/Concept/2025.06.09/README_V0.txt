Bliss Browser ChildWeb
2025.06.09: Call it Bliss Browser WebChild
A mode for Bliss Browser that locks the browser down, and makes it friendlier for children to use
All porn sites are blocked, while many sites (such as YouTube) are also blocked, due to them containing inappropriate content, and having an age requirement of 13-15 years

Sub modes

Toddler I (optimized for ages 2-3)
Toddler II (optimized for ages 3-5)
Child I (optimized for ages 5-7)
Child II (optimized for ages 7-11)
Child III (optimized for ages 11-12)
Teen I (optimized for ages 12-14)
Teen II (optimized for ages 14-17)

Users can customize the environment for these modes by blocking different sites on different modes
